# Lift & Cut 2.3.4 changelog

- Ingredient matching returns to the same scroll position after selecting a match or pressing Back.
- Recipe alternatives such as “Greek yogurt or sour cream” are presented as separate choices before nutrition matching.
- Preparation descriptions such as “finely chopped” and “freshly cracked” are removed from local and online search terms.
- Manual ingredient entry returns to the matching screen instead of closing the recipe workflow.
- Existing recipe and Google Sheets data remain compatible.
