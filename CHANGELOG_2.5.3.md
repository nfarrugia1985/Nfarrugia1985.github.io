# Lift & Cut 2.5.3 changelog

## Added
- Built-in **Modified MLM 6-Day Programme**.
- Six short sessions: MLMD1 Monday, MLMD2 Tuesday, MLMD3 Wednesday, Thursday rest, MLMD4 Friday, MLMD5 Saturday, MLMD6 Sunday.
- Exact set, rep and RIR prescriptions from the previously agreed modified programme.
- Exercise-cache entries required by the plan, including belt squat, tibialis raise, neck flexion/extension, cable hip abduction/adduction, wrist flexion/extension and hanging leg raise.
- Equipment choices for a belt-squat attachment and tibialis trainer.

## Compatibility
- Schema remains v6.
- Existing workouts, recipes, diet/RFL records and Google Sheets connection are unchanged.
- The new built-in programme is merged into existing phone databases at startup and is protected from deletion.
- No Apps Script or Google Sheets update is required.
