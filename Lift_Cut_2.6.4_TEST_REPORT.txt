Critical bug reproduced on v2.6.3
- Query: dry white wine
- v2.6.3 could rank Cheese, dry white and white/dry grains ahead of wine because adjective-only phrase overlap could outrank the food head.

Corrected behaviour in v2.6.4
- Search runs in progressive stages.
- Active phrase tokens must all be present in a returned USDA description.
- Phrases containing the right-most food-head term are tried before adjective-only phrases.
- Keyword fallback requires the fallback word to be present in the USDA food name.
- Lower-confidence widening is not mixed into a stronger result set.

Apps Script reliability
- Health-check timeout increased from 6.5 seconds to 15 seconds to reduce false unreachable messages caused by Apps Script cold starts/mobile latency.
- Failure text no longer implies local USDA matching depends on Apps Script.
- A true sync failure still requires checking More -> Cloud sync -> Test / deployment.
